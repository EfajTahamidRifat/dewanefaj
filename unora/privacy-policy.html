Unora · Android app

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026 · Last updated: 5 October 2026

In short

  • Unora works completely offline. Everything you create stays on your device.
  • We do not collect, store on servers, sell or share any of your data.
  • There is no account, login, advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • Data only leaves your device when you export or share it.

1Who we are

Unora ("the app", "we", "us") is a student productivity app for Android that combines notes, tasks, exams, a timetable, attendance, GPA/CGPA, a focus timer, habits, goals, flashcards, question banks, a document vault and calculators. It is developed by Efaj Tahamid, CEO of The Code Bros (thecodebros.xyz). This policy explains how the app handles information.

2Information we collect

We collect no personal information. The app has no servers and no backend. We never receive your notes, tasks, grades, attendance, study history, documents, device identifiers, location, contacts or any other information about you.

The app does not include any third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, crash reporting or social login, and it does not send data over the network.

3Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves what you enter in a private database on your device, including:

  • Your first name, if you choose to enter one (used only for the greeting on the home screen).
  • Subjects, notes, tasks, exams and syllabus checklists, timetable classes and attendance records.
  • Grades, credits and semesters for GPA/CGPA, and any grading systems you create.
  • Focus-timer study sessions, habits, goals, flashcards and review history, question banks and quiz results.
  • Copies of files you add to the document vault or attach to notes and tasks (for example PDFs or photos).
  • Your settings, such as theme, reminder preferences and dashboard layout.

This data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. It is never transmitted off the device by the app. The app also opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your data is not copied to any cloud account by the app.

4Permissions

Unora requests only the permissions it needs:

PermissionWhy
INTERNETRequired by Android's built-in web view component, which the app uses to display its own screens from files bundled inside the app. Unora does not use this permission to send or receive your data.
NotificationsOnly if you turn on reminders for classes, exams, tasks or the focus timer. Android asks you first, and you can turn it off at any time. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no push server is involved.
Run at startup / keep awakeLets scheduled reminders be restored after your phone restarts and delivered on time.
BiometricsOnly if you turn on fingerprint or face unlock for the app lock.

Unora does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo library. When you add a file, Android's system file picker opens and only the file you choose is shared with the app.

5App lock and private notes

If you set a PIN, the app stores only a salted, one-way hash of it (created with Android's built-in cryptography), never the PIN itself. Fingerprint and face unlock use Android's system biometric prompt: the app only receives a yes/no result and never sees or stores your biometric data. Notes you mark as private are hidden in the app until you unlock them.

6Backups and exports

You can export a full backup file (.unora), spreadsheets (CSV), notes (Markdown), flashcard decks and question banks. These files are created only when you ask. Android's share sheet or file picker opens and you choose where each file goes.

Backups can optionally be protected with a password, in which case the file is encrypted with AES-256. If you forget that password, the backup cannot be opened by you or by us. What happens to an exported file afterwards (for example, uploading it to a cloud drive or sending it to someone) is under your control and subject to the policies of the services you choose to use.

7Sharing and selling data

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any information with anyone, because we never have access to it.

8Deleting your data

  • Delete individual items inside the app. Deleted notes move to a trash and are removed permanently after 30 days, or when you empty the trash.
  • Use Settings → Delete all data to erase everything the app has stored.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all app data from the device.

Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover it once deleted. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.

9Security

Your data is protected by Android's app sandbox and your device's encryption. The optional app lock adds a privacy screen inside the app. Anyone who can unlock your phone and knows your Unora PIN (if set) can open your data, so we recommend also using a screen lock on your device.

10Links to other websites

If you add web links to your own notes and tap them, they open in your web browser, outside the app. Those websites have their own privacy practices.

11Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

12Purchases and Google Play

Unora is a paid app distributed through Google Play. Payment is processed entirely by Google; we never receive your payment details. Google may collect information when you purchase, download or update apps, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. That processing is done by Google, not by Unora.

13Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. If a future version of the app ever begins handling data differently, the policy will be updated before that version is released.
