Resolute Browser · Android app

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short

  • Resolute does not collect, sell or share your personal data. It has no server of its own.
  • Your history, tabs, bookmarks, passwords and settings stay on your phone.
  • No account, no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no tracking.
  • Resolute only connects to outside services when a feature needs them, and every one is listed below.

1Who we are

Resolute Browser ("Resolute", "the app", "we", "us") is a web browser for Android (package com.resolute.browser) that supports Chrome extensions and adds safety features for crypto and Web3 sites. It is developed by Dewan Efaj Tahamid Rifat and published on Google Play by The Code Bros (thecodebros.xyz).

Resolute is built on Chromium, the open-source project behind Google Chrome. Its source code is public at github.com/EfajTahamidRifat/resolute-browser, so anyone can check the statements in this policy.

2What stays on your phone

Everything you create while browsing is stored only on your device. We never receive it.

  • Browsing history, open tabs, bookmarks, downloads and saved pages.
  • Saved passwords, addresses and form entries (in Chromium's built-in password manager, or in the Android password app you choose).
  • Cookies and website data.
  • Settings, workspaces, pinned toggles and the data of the extensions you install.
  • Safety lists of known scam and phishing sites, downloaded to your phone.

You can delete this data at any time in Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data, or by uninstalling the app. Private (incognito) tabs are not saved to your history.

4Extensions

Extensions you install from the Chrome Web Store are made by other developers. They can read and change the websites you allow them to access, and their handling of data is covered by their own privacy policies, not this one. Resolute shows each extension's permissions before you install it, and you can remove an extension at any time.

5Crypto wallets and Web3

Resolute never holds your keys, seed phrase or funds, and never asks for them. Wallets such as MetaMask or Phantom run as extensions and keep your keys on your phone.

The two features below are planned for later versions. This section applies to them once they are released.

  • Transaction safety checks. When a site asks your wallet to sign something, Resolute may send the transaction details and your public wallet address to a public blockchain node (RPC) to preview what the transaction would do. Public addresses and transactions are visible on the blockchain anyway. You will be able to turn these checks off in Settings.
  • Swaps. If you use Resolute's swap feature, your public wallet address and the swap details are sent to the swap provider (LI.FI for most networks, Jupiter for Solana) to get prices and build the transaction. All fees, including Resolute's fee, are shown before you confirm.

6Resolute Picks and referral links

Resolute Picks are curated links shown in the app. Some contain our referral code, which may earn us a commission if you sign up; these are labelled. Resolute does not track whether you click them. The partner site sees your visit like any other website.

On some partner sign-up pages, Resolute may offer once to apply our referral code. It never replaces a code you already entered, and you can turn this off in Settings. Picks labelled "Made by Resolute's founder" (Neptuv and The Code Bros) are our own projects.

7Permissions

Resolute asks for Android permissions such as location, camera, microphone, notifications and files only when a website or feature needs them. Android asks you first, and you can refuse or remove any permission in Android settings.

8Sharing, selling and your rights

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any information with anyone, because we do not have it. There is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf: your data on the phone is under your control. For information held by one of the outside services listed above, please contact that service.

9Security

Resolute receives Chromium security updates regularly and uses Chromium's protections, such as site isolation and HTTPS. Its settings files are published from its public GitHub repository. Your data is also protected by Android's app sandbox and your device's encryption, so we recommend using a screen lock on your phone.

10Children's privacy

Resolute is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

11Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above, and mention the change in the app's release notes. If a future version of the app handles data differently, the policy will be updated before that version is released.
